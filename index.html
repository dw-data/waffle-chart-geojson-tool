DW Waffle Chart GeoJSON Generator

Please paste a list of IDs in the text field below.
There should be one row for each ID.
Each ID will be assigned to a square of the waffle chart.


This little tool produces a grid of square shapes, which will be downloaded as a GeoJSON file.
It’s designed for export to Datawrapper;
once uploaded you can treat the IDs as a data column and assign colors or other attributes to each square.

For further details on uploading a custom GeoJSON map, refer to step 6 of the Datawrapper guide.
